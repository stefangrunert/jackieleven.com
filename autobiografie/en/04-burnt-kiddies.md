## Documented / invented

**Documented (from Leven's own texts unless stated otherwise):** the squatted basement in Formosa Street W9, the heavy LSD use and the flat painted black, red and silver in the style of a Hammer horror set; the forming of Doll by Doll in this period and Leven's term “my art terrorist rock band” (Be My Friend, September 2011); the calendar with images of poverty, war and cruelty, delivered to the fifty most important people in the London music business by the road manager Colin, who threw the copies down in the reception areas; the postcards with the Japanese girl, the fox in the snare and the “gypsy tinsmith”, printed with “Doll By Doll Request”, “Regret”, “Have Moved”; the rejected album title “Burnt Kiddies” and Leven's “Shame” on the matter; the Reading Festival on heavy LSD, John Peel and Lemmy at the side of the stage and Du Noyer's NME line about the sun (all December 2006); the first concert Kris Needs saw, at the Acne Club in Aylesbury in front of about fifty people, “A nature ramble through Hell” and the question about the gig fees given away (ZigZag 84, June 1978); Jo Shaw smashing a £500 guitar at the ZigZag birthday party at the Venue, June 1979 (ZigZag 95); the chord sequence E–A–B–E–A–B, named “Summer's End” by Joe Shaw (September 2007).

**Invented:** all transitions; the image of the vampire as interior designer; the man in the good jacket; the reading that the aim was to make attention painful in a controlled way; the description of the band's sound, Jo Shaw's guitar playing and Dave McIntosh on drums; the weeping promoter; the closing thought about the one song since 1979.

**Caution:** Leven does not give the exact year of the Reading appearance; 1979 is inferred. The Needs review (ZigZag 84, June 1978) was held as zigzag84.htm in the fan site's press archive (see research/09-websites.md); its wording is not secured in the local corpus.
