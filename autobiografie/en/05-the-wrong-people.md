## Documented / invented

**Documented (from Leven's own texts unless stated otherwise):** the self-diagnosis “a reliable genius for seriously pissing off all the wrong people” together with “not everyone can do this” (August 2004); being thrown off the Devo tour after three days, the subsequent removal from the Hawkwind tour after the Hammersmith Odeon, the loss of the record deal “the week after” and Leven's verdict on Dave Brock (December 2006); the drive through Austria from Vienna to Innsbruck with the woman promoter, Leven's speech at breakfast, Nick Whiffen's prank with the underwear and the poster, “We never played Austria again”, Whiffen's comment “sorry about that - i don't know what came over me”, the 1000-mile drive through a snowstorm with the heating broken and the band being dropped by the record company on arrival (August 2004); Michael “Lord” Levy at Magnet Records (Be My Friend, December 2008); the punctual delivery of two albums and the cancellation of the largely sold-out tour after “traumatic rows” (NME, 15 May 1982); the unreleased fifth album “The Last Flick Of The Golden Wrench” (interview, 1995); Dave Gilmour on “Grand Passion” (March 2004); the contradictory break-up years 1982 and 1983 in Leven's own label biographies; Tony “Strat” Stratton Smith of Charisma (December 2008) and the solo deal during whose recordings the attack happened.

**Invented:** the forty harmless idiots in the room; the remark about Devo as flowerpots; the reading that the band ran on the principle of being beyond help; the paragraph about leaving things unsaid; his aside about the uncertain year; Strat's behaviour in this scene; the third row putting down their glasses.

**Caution:** Leven's own texts contradict each other on whether he was signed to Charisma, to Virgin or to both; the Telegraph names Chrysalis. The scene leaves this open.
