## Documented / invented

**Documented:** the location of the CORE Trust in Lisson Grove next to the social security office in Tresco House, near Paddington Green Police Station and the Seashell fish and chip restaurant; the row of cottages where previously “old ladies had been dumped there to die”; Leven's statement that he had lived there himself before the Trust was founded and was “actually part of the source of psychic infection”; the night vigil at the solstice with Jason Wright to “exorcise the building”, with “part of me just wanted to sleep and continue the distress” (all: column, December 2003); Leven's role as “head counsellor for The Core Trust” without formal training, and the objection of the trained counsellors (“you can't work here – you're not qualified”); the formula “the joke, the threat, the obvious”, which he took over from Max Rynish; the stages of his training: the Temenos Academy (Kathleen Raine), the London Convivium For Archetypal Studies (Noel Cobb, Eva Loewe), Applied Kinesiology with Brian Butler and the Koestler Foundation under Dr Ruth West, with the project on “the roots of coincidence” and African famine relief (Agony Aunt, September 2008); Max Rynish as a neighbour in the Formosa Street squat and his ruin through a campaign in Private Eye (Be My Friend, September 2011); the dinner party in Hampstead with the Schubert joke at which only Eva Loewe laughed (Be My Friend, September 2008); reading Bly from 1964 onwards in the school library in Glenrothes, and the poem “Poem Against The British”; Bly's five-week stay in Leven's house in Ladbroke Grove, Leven's year as “impromptu press officer”, the men's gatherings of up to 100 participants with fairy tales, sung poetry and bouzouki, and “a fabulous time of soul plenty, with a harvest of the hardest tears I'd ever shed” (Be My Friend, October 2009); “I am absolutely no Jungian – the idea of 'wholeness' makes me shudder”; leaving the Jungian dream group, and Hillman's remark about the black snake (column, March 2003).

**Invented:** the offer to fight anybody over the Seashell and the walk north with the parcel of fish and chips; “a brass plate on the door does nothing whatever about that”; the course of the night until breakfast (the old stories around four, the light over Lisson Grove at eight); the years 1986 and 1996 as the punchline about the word “holistic”; “wholeness … sounds like a diet”; the closing sentence about the songwriting starting again at this point.

**Caution:** Diana does not appear in Leven's own texts, and so she does not appear here either. That CORE was founded in 1985 cannot be documented from his texts; the year comes from the research compendium.
