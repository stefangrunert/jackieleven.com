## Documented / invented

**Documented:** Muff Winwood's interest and Feargal Sharkey's standard rejection with the wording “Sorry! – no Top Of The Pops for you this week!”; the encounter with Sharkey two years later, shortly after his dismissal, at a time when “a record … had just charted in Germany”; the collapse of Cooking Vinyl's distributor, Goldschmidt's candid offer of long-term artistic freedom, the two years until the signing and his quiet “Gotcha”; Leven's stage fright after the end of the band as “a trauma which I had to overcome” and the return to the stage at a music trade fair at the Quasimodo in Berlin (all: *Be My Friend*, December 2008); Goldschmidt's verdict on the number of albums and on Leven's “scams” (Guardian obituary); Robert Bly's two recordings, “The Garden” by Antonio Machado and “Clay Jug” by Kabir, at the Townhouse Studios (*Be My Friend*, October 2009); the two “In Loving Memory” pages in the booklet of “Fairytales for Hardmen” for Eva Loewe and Shannon Doyle, and the latter's role in supplying the images for “Lovers at the Gun Club”; Martin Okasili's vocals on “Mad As The Mist And Snow” and “Old West African Song”, recorded at the Milo Studios in Hoxton, and Leven's verdict on them (*Be My Friend*, September and December 2008); Haunted Valley as a joint venture with Mick Nolan, PO box in Belper, £14 a year, four issues (May, August, November, February), a free CD in the August mailing; “It will not be in the shops – cos there ARE no shops, or at least not many!” and “I'm a cottage industry and there's nobody in my cottage at the moment” (homepage, 2008); the music lawyer through whom the contact with Cooking Vinyl came about; “I am an abject failure” (Guardian, 26 July 2001).

**Invented:** the remark about typefaces and about his own vindictiveness; the image of the horse that has been hit; “It took Berlin twenty-four years and two lives to have another go at me”; the reading of the fan club as a “small, strange, unprofitable country”; the list Bergen–Bautzen–Brisbane; the closing paragraph with the arithmetic and the sentence about the business plan.

**Marked as legend:** Shannon Doyle as a contract killer is Leven's story, not biography. The chapter tells it as his story — including his refusal to take it back.
