## Documented / invented

**Documented:** the twelve flights a year with one airline; the tightly packed tours of Germany and Scandinavia with Michael Cosgrave and Kevin Foster and the tour manager whose name Leven himself spells inconsistently (“Gerne/Gernte Poets”); the tour stops in November 2005 from Solingen to Kiel; “Norway is like an extreme version of Scotland” (blog, December 2009); the Hamburg agency A.S.S. with Michael Bisping as its head, and the annual strategy meeting (column, April 2004); Frank as Scandinavian agent and festival director; Harry Farmer as booking contact on the website; the quarterly PRS payment of £22.43 when £600 was needed (column, May 2004); “you can make £1000 a night in merchandising, and over 20 dates, that really matters” (Triste interview, 2001); “the regenerative power of 'sudden community'” (blog, September 2008); the tribe at the Larmer Tree Festival with Mikki Rain as “Chieftan”, Joe Shaw and John Ralph as “tribal seer and Custodian-Of-Relics”; the three sets in Trowbridge after an hour's rehearsal in a site cabin; the view from the stage in Southsea to Hill Head, “where my mother died in a nursing home”; Manitoba 2009 with swine flu, jet lag, the funeral home “Sobering”, three shows and the song begun in Calgary, together with the comparison of musicians' cultures (“there is a melancholy to it”); “I have hysterics if I dislike my hotel room” (*Be My Friend*, October 2009); the growing dread of arriving in strange cities in the evening (column, October 2004); snoring as the reason for not sleeping on trains (December 2004); the Christmas concerts at the Milford Social Club “in the round” with Joe Shaw, Kevin Hewick and the Stornoway Girls, tickets at £8 through Mick Nolan, “a place that has been touched little by time”; the “Levens Lament” poured free on Burns Night at the Festival Club in Glasgow (column, November 2002) against Goldschmidt's “a complete scam” (Guardian obituary), and the photograph of the bottle on the website; “I find my job endlessly hard” and the sentence about picking up easily with people all over the world (blog, December 2009).

**Invented:** the contrast between German and British audiences (“and both things are true, and only one of them ever filled a room”); the comparison of Bisping with a Scottish farmer; the image of the village that exists for three days, and of the visitor who leaves as a cousin; the reading of the whisky night as “a party with a label on”; the envelope he kept; the closing remark about the single sentence and the airport.

**Caution:** Whether the whisky ever existed as a product is unresolved. The chapter leaves the question open, as Leven's own texts leave it open. The merchandising quote comes from the Triste interview (2001), whose wording is not secured in the local corpus.
