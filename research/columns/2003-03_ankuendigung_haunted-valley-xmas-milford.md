# Haunted Valley – The Jackie Leven Happy Xmas Get Together

Haunted Valley - The Jackie Leven Happy Xmas Get Together!

Venue: Milford Social Club, Milford, Derby (7 miles north of
Derby, A6 road, towards Belper). ( map )

Date: Saturday December 7th 2002. Doors: 7.15 p.m.

Ticket reservation: E-mail Mick Nolan at Mick@haunted-valley.freeserve.co.uk
or by phoning 01794 884695

Jackie will be having a Christmas get-together on December 7th 2002. The venue
will be Milford Social Club. I know this is an unlikely place for the gig, but
it's close to my place so we only have to take a few steps after the show, and
it's a place that has been touched little by time! The concert will in the round.
It should be a great night, so don't miss it!
