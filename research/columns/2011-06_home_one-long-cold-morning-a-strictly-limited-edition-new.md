# ONE LONG COLD MORNING -a strictly limited edition new…

ONE LONG COLD MORNING -a strictly limited edition new Jackie Leven album in the honourable tradition of Cooking Vinyl approved 'Official Bootlegs'.

ONE LONG COLD MORNING contains five brand new studio recordings, including the songs 'The Snow That Thought It Was A Dream' and 'The View From Shit Creek'.

Also included are live band and solo recordings from different places plus typically hilarious storytelling, and rarities such as a poignant stage version of his majestic interpretation of 'I Say A Little Prayer'.

The album is available for pre-order now and will be shipped from June 18th(Jackie's birthday, but not thankfully by Jackie!) First 150 copies ordered will be signed by Jackie but we are expecting this to sell out so please don't rely on this being available at live shows....
